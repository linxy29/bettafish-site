MODULE 01Available

Groups & Collaboration

The base layer

The base layer: a group's people, papers and discussion in one place.

  • Create groups, invite members, assign roles
  • Papers filed in folders and shared across the group
  • Read online, with highlights and annotations visible to everyone
  • Meeting notes, research plans, progress reports and conference tracking
MODULE 02Available

Reading Assistant

AI-assisted reading

Turns "finished one paper" into "got through a stack".

  • Fast overview and key-point extraction
  • Ask questions about the full text, answers anchored to the passage
  • Compare several papers side by side
  • Bilingual close reading with figures kept in place

  • Export to EndNote / BibTeX / RIS

Read the same paper, see each other's thinking

Highlights and annotations are shared across the group. Skimming what everyone marked beats re-reading the paper before a meeting — and what you argued about afterwards stays next to the text instead of sinking into a chat log.
